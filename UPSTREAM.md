# Upstream attribution

DataPilot is a derivative project based on the open-source DeepAnalyze repository:

- Upstream repository: https://github.com/ruc-datalab/DeepAnalyze
- Upstream project: DeepAnalyze: Agentic Large Language Models for Autonomous Data Science
- Upstream authors: Shaolei Zhang, Ju Fan, Meihao Fan, Guoliang Li, and Xiaoyong Du
- Upstream institutions: Renmin University of China and Tsinghua University
- Upstream license: MIT

The initial DataPilot baseline preserves substantial portions of the upstream
source code, including the WebUI, API, execution environment, examples, training
framework integrations, and evaluation resources. The original copyright and
license notice remain in `LICENSE`.

DataPilot's independent work is being developed incrementally and will include
branding, model-provider configuration, deployment packaging, tests, documentation,
and an online portfolio demonstration. Git history is used to keep those changes
auditable.

DeepAnalyze-8B and its trained parameters are upstream model artifacts. They were
not trained by the DataPilot maintainer and are not stored in this Git repository.
